# Indigenous Diabetes Wellness Resource Hub — Design Brainstorm

## Context
This is a comprehensive, institutional resource hub for Indigenous peoples navigating diabetes wellness. Affiliated with Civic Firm (institutional digital partner). The site must feel authoritative, trustworthy, and genuinely useful — not a brochure, but a real destination people return to. It should honour Indigenous identity without being tokenistic or decorative.

**Idea 1: Land-Based Modernism**

**Design Movement:** The aesthetic language of the land itself — soil strata, birchbark texture, river stone, midnight sky. Institutional weight meets organic warmth.

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep, grounded colour — no pastels. Dark earth tones anchor the page.
2. Editorial hierarchy — large bold category labels; readable body; subtle metadata.
3. Resource-first layout — the site is a tool, not a story. Navigation is immediate and functional.
4. Texture as identity — subtle grain overlays and organic shapes replace decorative illustration.

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Deep forest `#1A2E1A` (trust, land, permanence)
- Secondary: Warm amber `#C8842A` (fire, energy, action)
- Background: Pale birch `#F0EBE0` (paper, clarity, breath)
- Text: Near-black `#1C1C1C`
- Muted: Slate sage `#4A5E4A`
These are the colours of the boreal forest at dusk — not decorative, but deeply felt.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Full-width editorial columns. Left-heavy asymmetry. Large section labels in the left margin. Split-screen hero: dark left panel (title + stats) + full-bleed image right panel. No centred hero.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Horizontal rule in amber — separates major sections
2. Large spaced uppercase category labels in the left margin
3. Resource cards with a left-border accent in amber

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif, editorial authority)
- Body: Source Sans 3 (clean, accessible, institutional)
- Labels: Space Mono (monospace, data-like precision)

**Idea 2: Arctic Blueprint**

**Design Movement:** The visual language of technical documents, cartography, and Indigenous beadwork geometry. Clean, precise, and deeply respectful.

**Core Principles:**
1. Blueprint grid — subtle grid lines as background texture, evoking maps and technical plans
2. Geometric motifs from beadwork — repeating diamond/chevron patterns as section dividers
3. High contrast — near-white backgrounds with very dark type for maximum readability
4. Data-forward — statistics and facts displayed prominently as large typographic elements

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Midnight navy `#0D1B2A`
- Secondary: Copper `#B5651D`
- Background: Ice white `#F8F9FA`
- Highlight: Turquoise `#2E8B8B` (water, sky, healing)

**Layout Paradigm:**
Strict vertical rhythm. Wide left margin for section numbers and labels. Statistics displayed as full-width "data moments" between content sections.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (elegant, historical weight)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (technical, accessible)
- Labels: IBM Plex Mono

**Idea 3: Warm Institutional**

**Design Movement:** The aesthetic of the best public health publications — serious but human, data-rich but readable, institutional but not cold.

**Core Principles:**
1. Warmth through restraint — warm off-white base with one strong accent colour
2. Clear information hierarchy — resources are scannable, filterable, and immediately actionable
3. Community voice — pull quotes treated as primary content, not decoration
4. Accessibility-first — high contrast, large type, clear focus states throughout

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Charcoal `#2C2C2C`
- Secondary: Burnt sienna `#A0522D`
- Background: Warm white `#FAF8F5`
- Accent: Sage green `#5C7A5C`
- Highlight: Pale gold `#E8C97A`
These are the colours of well-worn books, cedar, and sunlight through leaves.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Generous margins. Left-aligned everything. Resources in a filterable card grid. Hero is a full-width banner with a strong typographic statement. Sections alternate between white and warm-off-white backgrounds.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Amber/gold left-border on resource cards
2. Large pull-quote blocks with oversized quotation marks
3. Section dividers using a thin amber rule + spaced uppercase label

**Typography System:**
- Display: DM Serif Display (warm, editorial)
- Body: Lato (clean, readable, widely accessible)
- Labels: Space Mono (technical precision)

## Selected Approach: **Idea 3 — Warm Institutional**

This approach delivers the institutional authority the site needs while staying genuinely warm and human. Charcoal + burnt sienna + sage green on a warm white base. DM Serif Display for headings, Lato for body, Space Mono for labels and data. Left-aligned editorial layout with filterable resource cards, pull-quote blocks, and amber-bordered section dividers. This is the aesthetic of a well-designed public health publication — trustworthy, readable, and built to be used.
